# Jarvis Roadmap

Planning repository for a locally operated whole-home voice assistant and multiroom audio system.

- **[Open the comprehensive roadmap](ROADMAP.md)** — architecture, hardware, software, music streams, safety, phases, acceptance tests, budget planning, risks, and initial task backlog.

## Project baseline

- 12 audio zones, 4 ceiling speakers per zone (48 total).
- 6 voice-enabled zones and 6 speaker-only zones.
- 24 amplifier channels for 12 stereo zones.
- Local-first voice pipeline with fast/strong model routing and streaming TTS.
- Multiroom playback plus independent music streams and a shared Jarvis taste profile.
- Prototype and validate before full-house procurement.

The roadmap is a living design document. Hardware and provider choices remain provisional until they pass the stated prototype gates.
